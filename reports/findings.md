
Findings
Financial corporations held €12,193.06 billion in financial assets at year-end 2025, the largest amount among the four individual sectors compared.
Their financial assets increased from €10,555.39 billion in 2015 to €12,193.06 billion in 2025, a 15.52% nominal increase. This is balance-sheet growth, not an investment return or profit measure.
Equity and investment fund shares represented 41.21% of financial-corporation assets in 2025; loans represented 30.84%. These are the two largest categories shown for that sector.
Method
Source: user-supplied CBS 85883ENG CSV export, cleaned to 390 observations and 19 columns. Charts use closing balance sheets only. Sector comparisons use 2025*; the annual trend uses 2015–2025 and excludes quarterly observations. Amounts are converted from million euros to billion euros by dividing by 1,000. The aggregate Total domestic sectors is excluded from sector comparisons. Asset composition includes a residual category so each sector sums to 100%.
Interpretation limits
Figures are not consolidated and include positions within sectors. Financial corporations include more than banks.
The 2025 asterisk is preserved from the supplied source. Confirm its definition in CBS metadata before describing revision status in published work.
No inflation adjustment is applied. Changes can reflect transactions, revaluations and other changes.
Financial assets and the financial asset/liability balance do not measure total wealth including non-financial assets.
Raw data are preserved. Missing observations are not replaced with zeros.
